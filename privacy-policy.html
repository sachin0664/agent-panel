Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sathi Pay handles information when customers register for and use the Sathi Pay platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Sathi Pay may collect and process:

  • Name, mobile number, email address, and customer UID.
  • Account and authentication information required to operate your account.
  • UPI ID and related UPI information that you choose to add to the platform.
  • Deposit information such as amount, UTR, transaction details, and payment screenshots or other supporting information submitted by you.
  • Referral, commission, wallet, transaction, and account activity information.
  • Technical information needed for security, troubleshooting, and service operation, such as browser/device information, logs, and approximate usage information where available.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your Sathi Pay account.
  • To authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • To process and review deposits, wallet transactions, referrals, commissions, and other requested services.
  • To prevent fraud, duplicate transactions, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • To provide customer support and respond to service requests.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the platform.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, security, or law-enforcement requirements.

3. Payment and UPI Information

Sathi Pay may process the payment and UPI information necessary to provide the features you request. Customers should submit only information that is required for the relevant transaction and should never share an OTP, password, UPI PIN, or other confidential authentication credential with another person.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information when reasonably necessary to operate the platform, provide technical or service support, process requested services, protect users and the platform, investigate suspected fraud or abuse, or comply with applicable law or a lawful request. We do not treat customer information as public information merely because a customer uses the platform.

5. Data Security

Sathi Pay uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction and account records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, meet security requirements, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies for login state, referral information, security, preferences, and basic service functionality. You can control certain browser storage or cookie settings through your device or browser, although disabling required storage may affect some features.

8. Your Responsibilities

  • Keep your password and UPI PIN confidential.
  • Provide accurate information and update it when necessary.
  • Do not upload or submit information that you do not have the right to provide.
  • Notify Sathi Pay support if you suspect unauthorized access or misuse of your account.

9. Third-Party Services

Sathi Pay may rely on third-party infrastructure or service providers to support authentication, hosting, communications, payments, security, analytics, or other platform functions. Such providers may process information only as necessary for the services they provide and subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

10. Children's Privacy

The Sathi Pay platform is intended for users who are legally permitted to enter into the relevant service agreement under applicable law. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The latest version published on the platform will apply from its stated update date.

12. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning your Sathi Pay account information, use the official Sathi Pay customer support option provided on the platform. We may need to verify your account before processing certain requests.

13. Acceptance

By registering for and using Sathi Pay, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and understand how information may be handled as described above.
